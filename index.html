Editor's Note

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

I came to data science the way most people come to a good newspaper — for the answers, and then I stayed for the methods. What pulls me in is the gap between what a model knows and what a decision-maker understands. Accuracy is table stakes; the real work is translation.

On the churn project that meant pairing XGBoost with SHAP so a retention team could see, customer by customer, which terms of service actually tipped people out the door. On the crowd-analytics side it meant density maps a safety officer can read at a glance, not a wall of logits. Prediction without explanation is a rumor with a confidence score.

“ A model nobody can explain is a rumor with a confidence score.

Right now I am an undergraduate in data science, which mostly means I get excited about things the industry has already declared boring — and then find out the industry was wrong. I work in Python and SQL first, reach for XGBoost and SHAP when the question deserves them, and prototype enough JavaScript to put a demo in a stranger's browser.

Off the clock: reading papers I will never be examined on, defending the semicolon, and checking the wave forecast more often than is reasonable.

01

TELECOM · CHURN ANALYTICS · EXPLAINABLE AI

Why customers leave — explained, not just predicted→

An XGBoost churn model on the IBM Telco dataset, paired with SHAP values so every prediction comes with its reasons attached.

The model flags the customers at risk; the SHAP analysis is the story worth reading — which contract terms, charges and tenure actually move the needle. Built so a retention team could act on the numbers without taking the model on faith.

  • XGBoost
  • SHAP
  • Python
  • IBM Telco

02

COMPUTER VISION · CROWD ANALYTICS

Counting crowds, in real time→

A surveillance framework that fuses YOLOv8 detection with CSRNet density estimation to count people where counting people is hard.

Detection handles the sparse frames; density estimation covers the packed ones the detector gives up on. The result is a pipeline that keeps up with live video and gives safety teams a number they can trust when the frame is shoulder-to-shoulder.

  • YOLOv8
  • CSRNet
  • OpenCV
  • Python
  • Real-time
03

COASTAL · TIME SERIES · FORECASTING

Predicting the sea's sudden moods→

WaveGuard — an early-warning predictor for Kallakkadal, the sudden-onset swell waves that flood the Indian coast with little notice.

Kallakkadal waves arrive without the usual courtesy of a storm nearby, which is exactly why they are dangerous. WaveGuard watches the ocean variables that precede them and raises a flag early — the difference between a coastline that scrambles and one that simply moves up the beach.

  • JavaScript
  • Time series
  • Forecasting
  • Early warning

Verifai — checking the machine's homework→

An experiment in verification: tooling that interrogates model output instead of taking it at face value.

If explainability is opening the hood, verification is taking the car for a drive with a clipboard. The project lives in my GitHub and is developing quickly — the wire below carries the latest.
