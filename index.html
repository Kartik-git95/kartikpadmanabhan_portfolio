About

Comfortable in the space between a spreadsheet and a stakeholder.

I'm a Data Science undergraduate at Mar Athanasius College of Engineering, building the habits of an analyst who doesn't just model — I make sure the model can be trusted, questioned, and explained.

My recent work sits at two ends of the same problem: on one side, predicting which telecom customers are about to churn and surfacing exactly why through SHAP explainability; on the other, routing live camera feeds to the right computer-vision model depending on how crowded a scene actually is. Both come down to the same instinct — pick the right tool for the data in front of you, and never ship a number without a reason attached.

Outside coursework, I've run through GenAI-powered analytics workflows with Tata and Deloitte's simulation programs, and placed in the top 12 of 60 teams at .hack() 2025. I'm now looking for a Data Analyst role where that instinct gets pointed at real business questions.
